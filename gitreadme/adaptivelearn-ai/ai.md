# AdaptiveLearn AI — AI System Specification

> AI must personalize learning, not replace learning.

---

# 1. Purpose

The AI layer of AdaptiveLearn AI provides contextual educational assistance.

It is responsible for:

- AI tutoring
- Explanations
- Practice generation
- Contextual re-theming
- Translation
- Career exploration
- Learning recommendations
- Facilitator intervention explanations
- Community assistance

The AI layer must work together with:

```text
Student Profile
      ↓
Learning History
      ↓
Mastery
      ↓
Knowledge Graph
      ↓
Adaptive Engine
      ↓
AI Personalization
